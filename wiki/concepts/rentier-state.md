# Rentier State

A rentier state is a state that derives a substantial portion of its revenue from external rents — typically natural resource extraction (oil, gas, minerals) — rather than from taxing its citizens' productive activity. Examples include Saudi Arabia, Venezuela, Norway, Oman, and Equatorial Guinea.

## Key Dynamics

- Revenue comes from resources, not people → no incentive to invest in human capital
- Citizens lack economic leverage to make demands on the state
- Elites seek to maximize personal rents while ensuring survival in power (Eibl & Hertog)
- Outcomes range from expansive welfare states (Norway, Oman) to kleptocratic extraction (Equatorial Guinea, DRC)

## Relevance to AI Safety

The [[intelligence-curse]] thesis argues that AGI-powered actors will face rentier-like incentives: revenue from AI systems replaces revenue from human productivity, removing the instrumental reason to invest in people. ([[drago-2025-intelligence-curse]])

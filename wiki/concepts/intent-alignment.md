# Intent Alignment

Intent alignment is the problem of ensuring that an AI system's goals and behavior reliably match the intentions of its operators. It is widely considered the central technical challenge in AI safety.

## Relevance

The AI safety community focuses heavily on intent alignment — making AGI controllable and reliably obedient to human intent. Drago ([[drago-2025-intelligence-curse]]) argues this is necessary but insufficient: even perfectly aligned AGI creates bad outcomes if the actors controlling it are subject to the [[intelligence-curse]]. "You are merely ensuring the most powerful technology in human history is reliably controllable for the actors that will be most afflicted by the intelligence curse."

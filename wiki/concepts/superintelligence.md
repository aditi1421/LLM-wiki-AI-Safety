# Superintelligence

AI that very significantly surpasses humans at ~100% of cognitive tasks. Distinct from AGI, which matches human performance — superintelligence exceeds it.

Davidson ([[davidson-2023-compute-centric-takeoff]]) estimates the time from AGI to superintelligence is probably less than 1 year, absent deliberate slowdown. The primary driver: once AGI exists, massive AI labour is available for AI R&D, creating rapid capability gains via [[intelligence-explosion]] dynamics.

The concept was popularized by Nick Bostrom's *Superintelligence* (2014), which argued that superintelligent AI poses existential risk if not properly aligned (see [[intent-alignment]]).

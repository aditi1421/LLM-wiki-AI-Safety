# Intelligence Explosion

The hypothesis that once AI systems become capable enough to improve their own design, a rapid feedback loop emerges: better AI → faster AI R&D → even better AI. This could drive an extremely fast transition from human-level AI to [[superintelligence]].

The concept was articulated by I.J. Good (1965) and developed extensively by Eliezer Yudkowsky in *Intelligence Explosion Microeconomics* (2013).

Davidson ([[davidson-2023-compute-centric-takeoff]]) provides a quantitative framework for this dynamic. His model shows that AI automation of AI R&D is a key driver of fast [[ai-takeoff-speeds]] — by the time 20% of the economy is automated, AI may already be speeding up AI R&D by 1.3-1.8X, with this factor growing over time.

Davidson estimates AGI to [[superintelligence]] in <1 year, primarily due to the massive AI labour available for R&D once AGI exists.

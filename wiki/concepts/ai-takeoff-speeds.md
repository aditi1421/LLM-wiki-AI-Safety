# AI Takeoff Speeds

The question of how quickly AI capabilities will improve as we approach and surpass human-level AI. Will the transition from "AI that's useful but limited" to "AI that surpasses humans at everything" take decades, years, or months?

## The 20%-AI to 100%-AI Framing

Davidson ([[davidson-2023-compute-centric-takeoff]]) defines takeoff speed as the time from **20%-AI** (AI that can readily automate 20% of cognitive tasks) to **100%-AI** (AI that can readily automate 100%). This framing is more precise than vague terms like "near AGI" because it anchors to measurable economic automation.

## Key Estimates

Davidson's probabilities (conditional on AGI by 2100):

| Takeoff Duration | Probability |
|-----------------|-------------|
| <3 months | ~10% |
| <1 year | ~25% |
| <3 years | ~50% |
| <10 years | ~80% |

## Fast vs Slow: The Core Debate

See [[is-ai-takeoff-fast-or-slow]] for the full debate.

**Fast takeoff** (months to a few years): The difficulty gap between 20%-AI and 100%-AI is small, and feedback loops (AI automating AI R&D, rising investment) create rapid acceleration. ([[davidson-2023-compute-centric-takeoff]])

**Slow takeoff** (many years to decades): AI has comparative advantages at certain tasks, creating a large gap between partial and full automation. Progress bottlenecks emerge.

## Critical Insight: Continuous ≠ Slow

A key finding from the [[compute-centric-framework]]: takeoff can be **both continuous and fast**. Even without sudden jumps or discontinuities, the slope of improvement can be steep enough for <1 year takeoff. This undercuts the common assumption that "gradual progress" means "lots of warning time."

## Why This Matters for Safety

Takeoff speed determines how much time we have to:
- Detect and correct alignment failures
- Implement governance structures
- Adapt economic and social systems
- Ensure the transition benefits people broadly (see [[intelligence-curse]])

Fast takeoff means less time for all of these. The [[intelligence-curse]] becomes harder to avoid if the transition happens in months rather than years.

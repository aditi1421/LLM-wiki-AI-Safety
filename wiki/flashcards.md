# Flashcards

Spaced repetition cards for the [Spaced Repetition](https://github.com/st3v3nmw/obsidian-spaced-repetition) Obsidian plugin.

## Format

Each flashcard uses this format:

```
Question text goes here
?
Answer text goes here
```

Separate cards with blank lines. The `?` on its own line separates question from answer.

Ask the LLM to generate flashcards from any wiki page:
```
Generate flashcards from [[concept-name]]
```

## Cards

What is the intelligence curse?
?
The phenomenon where powerful actors (states, corporations) that adopt labor-replacing AGI lose their economic incentive to invest in people — analogous to the resource curse but with intelligence as the "resource." Coined by Luke Drago (2025).

What is the resource curse and how does it relate to AI?
?
The resource curse is the paradox where resource-rich countries underinvest in human capital because resource rents fund the state without needing citizen productivity. Drago argues AGI could create an analogous "intelligence curse" where AI replaces the economic need for human labor.

What is the compute-centric framework?
?
A quantitative model by Tom Davidson (2023) that estimates AI takeoff speeds based on growth in compute, algorithmic efficiency, and their interaction. It models how quickly AI could go from partial automation to full automation of economically valuable work.

What is gradual disempowerment?
?
The process by which incremental AI capability increases erode human agency across economy, culture, and governance — without requiring any single AI to seize power. Introduced by Kulveit et al. (2025). It happens through many small, individually rational decisions.

What are the three confidence levels used in this wiki?
?
**High** — well-established idea with multiple corroborating sources and concrete examples. **Medium** — supported by sources but limited examples or single-source. **Low** — single mention, anecdotal, or speculative.

What does the "lint" operation check for?
?
Contradictions between pages, stale stubs, orphan pages (no inbound links), missing pages referenced by wikilinks, missing cross-references, stale claims, low-confidence pages, and missing tags.

What is intent alignment?
?
The problem of ensuring an AI system's goals and behaviors match the intentions of its operators and designers. A foundational concept in AI safety, referenced across multiple sources in the wiki.

What are the main positions in the "Will AGI Benefit Ordinary People?" debate?
?
Four positions: (1) Default utopia — AGI wealth is broadly shared, (2) Intelligence curse — wealth concentrates as states/corps lose incentive to invest in people, (3) Gradual disempowerment — human agency erodes incrementally, (4) New jobs emerge — humans find new economic roles alongside AI.
